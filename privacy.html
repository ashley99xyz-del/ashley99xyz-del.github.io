Privacy Policy

Atlas of Almost · Effective 6 October 2026

Who we are

Atlas of Almost publishes short history videos. This policy covers the Atlas of Almost website and the Atlas of Almost publishing tool ("Atlas poster"), which uses the YouTube API Services and other platform APIs.

What the publishing tool does

The tool is used only by the Atlas of Almost team to manage the Atlas of Almost channels: it sets video details (category, audience, playlists, subtitles), posts the channel's own comments and reads the channel's own analytics. It does not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube user or channel.

Data we collect

We collect no personal data from visitors to this website or from viewers of our videos. The tool keeps an internal log of the actions it took on our own videos (video ID, action and time). It contains no viewer data.

Google user data

The tool's access to Google user data is limited to the Atlas of Almost YouTube account that authorised it. That data is used only to manage that account, is never sold or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking.
